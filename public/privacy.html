Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

UNIQORA respects your privacy. This policy explains the information we collect when you use our store, why we use it, and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect information such as your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, city, pincode, order details and payment status. We may also receive basic technical information needed for website security and operation.

2. How We Use Information

We use customer information to process and deliver orders, confirm payments, provide customer support, communicate important order updates, prevent misuse or fraud, maintain business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Razorpay. UNIQORA does not store your full card number, UPI PIN or other confidential payment credentials. Payment providers process payment information under their own privacy and security practices.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share only the information reasonably necessary with service providers involved in fulfilling your order, such as payment processors, hosting providers and delivery or courier partners. We do not sell customer personal information.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfilment, customer support, accounting, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and legal compliance. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

The website may use browser storage or similar technologies for essential shopping functions such as remembering cart contents and improving the shopping experience.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us regarding your personal information or privacy questions. Certain records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, accounting, dispute or legal purposes.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.
